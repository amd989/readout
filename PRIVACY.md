# Privacy policy

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Readout does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- **Bluetooth.** The app uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your multimeter. It does not
  use Bluetooth to find your location.
- **Readings.** Measurements stay on your device. They leave it only when you export a CSV
  file and choose where to send it.
- **Settings.** The app remembers the last meter you connected to, on your device only.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app makes no network connections.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/amd989/121gw/issues>.
